Why Timetable Optimization Matters

  • 1
    The Real-World Problem: Students juggle multiple subjects with competing deadlines, varying difficulty priorities, and limited daily focus hours.
  • 2
    Why Algorithms Matter: Naive manual planning leads to last-minute cramming and overlapping sessions. DAA optimization maximizes priority-weighted learning before deadlines.
  • 3
    How It Works: Enter your subjects and daily constraints below. Our Flask backend runs all four classical paradigms and benchmarks their optimality, speed, and complexity.
Paradigm 01

Brute Force

Exhaustively enumerates every valid study-hour combination to guarantee the exact global optimum.

Time: O((R_max + 1)^N)
Paradigm 02

Greedy Algorithm

Selects subjects with the highest urgency-weighted priority ratio first for near-instant scheduling.

Time: O(N log N + T·N)
Paradigm 03

Dynamic Programming

Formulates allocation as a Bounded Knapsack DP table, reusing overlapping subproblem results.

Time: O(N × C × R_max)
Paradigm 04

Backtracking

Assigns subjects slot-by-slot across days, pruning invalid branches and backtracking on conflicts.

Time: O(N^(T·H)) Pruned
